# ShopSense

ShopSense is a Multi-Vendor E-Commerce Analytics Platform that provides a complete marketplace for customers, vendors, and administrators. It combines e-commerce functionality with sales analytics, inventory management, customer segmentation, sales forecasting, and product recommendations.

## Features

### Customer
- Customer registration and login
- Browse and view products
- Product details
- Shopping cart
- Wishlist
- Checkout and order placement
- Order history
- Product recommendations

### Vendor
- Vendor registration and login
- Product management
- Add and edit products
- Inventory management
- Transaction tracking
- Sales reports
- Inventory analytics
- Sales forecasting
- AI dashboard

### Administrator
- Admin dashboard
- Vendor approval and rejection
- Vendor management
- Customer analytics
- Customer segmentation
- Sales reports
- Sales forecasting
- Platform analytics

## Analytics Features

### Sales Forecasting
Historical transaction and sales data is analyzed to estimate future sales and help with business planning.

### Customer Segmentation
Customer purchasing behavior is analyzed to divide customers into meaningful groups based on their activity and purchasing patterns.

### Product Recommendations
Relevant products are recommended to customers to improve product discovery and the shopping experience.

### Inventory Analytics
Inventory and sales information is analyzed to identify low-stock and out-of-stock products and support restocking decisions.

## Project Architecture

ShopSense follows a frontend-backend architecture:

- React provides the user interface.
- FastAPI provides REST API endpoints.
- SQLAlchemy handles database operations.
- MySQL stores users, products, orders, transactions, and other application data.
- Analytics and machine learning techniques provide business insights and sales forecasting.

## User Roles

1. **Customer** - Browses products, manages cart and wishlist, places orders, and receives product recommendations.
2. **Vendor** - Manages products, inventory, transactions, reports, analytics, and sales forecasting.
3. **Administrator** - Manages vendors and monitors customers, sales, segmentation, forecasting, and platform analytics.
